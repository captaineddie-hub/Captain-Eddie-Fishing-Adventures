--- DESCRIPTION ---
THE BITE IS ON. THE WEATHER IS REAL. THE LEADERBOARD IS LIVE.

Salt & Sound Sportfishing puts a rod in your hands on Topsail Island, North Carolina. Your host is Captain Eddie, a real Surf City captain. The wind, waves, water temperature and tide come from today's actual Topsail conditions. If the drum are running on the island, they're running in the game.

Feel every fish. Cast in first person, then flip to the underwater view and watch a red drum find your bait, nose it, tap it... and take off. Fish run, jump, dive for the pilings and wear down on their own. Your drag, line and nerve decide whether you land it or snap off. It's the "one more cast" feel of the classic Black Bass and Saltwater Sportfishing games, rebuilt with modern fight physics.

COMPETE EVERY DAY
- DAILY SURF DERBY + DAILY PIER DERBY: new tournaments every day, open to everyone
- WEEKEND CLASSICS: the Topsail Surf Classic and the Surf City Pier Classic
- FAIR WATER: every angler fishes the same seeded conditions. Your best 3 fish count, and every catch is weighed in live
- LIVE LEADERBOARDS: watch the standings move in real time, in the game or on the web
- PRIVATE LEAGUES: start a league, text the code to your buddies, and trash-talk in league chat while you fish
- TIMED CHALLENGES: Bluefish Blitz, Slot Red, Spot Run, King at the End, Birds Working, Wreck Run. Beat the clock for bonus points and cash
- PLAY ANYWHERE: sign in with just your email (a 6-digit code, no password) and your progress follows you from phone to tablet to PC

FOUR WAYS TO FISH
PIER: Three real Topsail piers: Surf City, Jolly Roger and Seaview. Walk the deck, pick your spot at the rail, and drop for sheepshead on the pilings or soak a bait off the end for kings and big drum. Your rods stay in the holders while you walk around, and other anglers fish the rail beside you.
SURF: Drive the Jeep down the beach, read the sloughs, rips and birds, and plant your rods in the trough for red drum, bluefish, pompano, Spanish, spot and croaker. Slide your fish up the wash on a wave.
OFFSHORE: Run to the Gulf Stream weed lines and the real wrecks off Topsail. When the clicker screams, GRAB THE ROD and go to work on mahi, wahoo, kings, yellowfin, sailfish and blue marlin while the captain backs down.
LAKE: Run the bass boat across Pine Ridge Reservoir, throw poppers, cranks, worms and jigs at docks, timber and lily pads, and fill a 5-fish limit before weigh-in.
