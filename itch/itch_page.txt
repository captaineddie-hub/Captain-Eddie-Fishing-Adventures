--- DESCRIPTION ---
Grab your rod and climb aboard with Captain Eddie, a Carolina coast storyteller, chaos manager and wildlife whisperer.

Captain Eddie Fishing Adventures brings back the "one more cast" feel of two classic fishing games, The Black Bass and Saltwater Sportfishing, on modern fish-fight physics. Cast in first person with the rod in your hands. Then switch views anytime: ROD, BELOW (underwater) or ABOVE (overhead). Watch the fish notice your lure, follow it, inspect it, nip it, and strike or spook. Every fish runs, jumps, dives for cover and tires out on its own. Your drag, line and timing decide whether it hits the livewell or snaps you off.

LAKE: BASS TOUR
Run the boat across Pine Ridge Reservoir and cast to docks, timber, rock piles, weed beds and lily pads. Work a popper, crankbait, spinnerbait, worm or jig. Largemouth, smallmouth, spotted bass, crappie and catfish live their own lives under the surface. Read their reactions from above or below and set the hook on the bite. Fill a 5-fish limit before weigh-in.

OFFSHORE: GULF STREAM TROLLING
Run to the weed lines and current edge, set your spread and watch the finder and birds. When the clicker screams, GRAB THE ROD, then pump and wind while the captain backs down. Fight mahi, wahoo, kingfish, yellowfin, sailfish and blue marlin, then gaff at the boat.

SURF: TOPSAIL FALL RUN
Drive the Jeep anywhere on the beach, around and under the pier. Read the sloughs, rips and birds, plant your rods and cast into the troughs for red drum, bluefish, pompano, Spanish mackerel and more. Tap to crank, and slide your fish up the wash on a wave. Ghost crabs, sanderlings, pelicans, mullet and dolphins share the beach with you.

ONLINE SURF TOURNAMENTS
A Daily Surf Derby every day and the Topsail Surf Classic every weekend. Everyone fishes the same seeded water, every fish you land is weighed in live, and your best 3 fish count. Create a private league, text the invite code to your crew, and chat during the tournament. Live leaderboards: captain-eddie-tournaments.lovable.app

FEATURES
- First-person casting and fights, plus ROD / BELOW / ABOVE views like the classics
- Fish AI you can read: notice, follow, inspect, nip, strike, spook
- Real 3D fish for 20 species, and a catch card for every fish
- Fish-fight physics: drag, line strength, pumping and side pressure all matter
- Tackle shop, tournament ladder, fish book and trophy room
- Online tournaments, private leagues and chat
- Play on phone, tablet, desktop or TV (screen mirroring). Play in landscape.

CONTROLS
Touch: on-screen buttons. Keyboard: WASD drive · Space reel · F twitch · W lift rod · [ ] drag · Enter cast / set hook · 1 2 3 or V switch views
